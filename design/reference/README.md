# Design reference

Exports of the approved homepage design (direction C) for anyone who cannot open the Kempyre design canvas.

- `homepage-c-desktop.jpg`: the full homepage at 1440px wide.
- `homepage-c-phone.jpg`: the full homepage at 390px wide (one long scroll; on the real site the photo rows and the team slide sideways).
- `Pegasus_Website_Homepage_Design_Preview_v1.1.pdf`: the review document for the clinic (v1.1, 14 October 2026: logo v1.4 and the "Find us" label; v1.0 of 9 October carried the superseded v1.2 logo and must not be used); pages 3–9 are the design with captions, page 10 explains how the site behaves.

Every photograph in these files is a stock stand-in and every word is draft. The numbers behind the design are in `../tokens.json`; the rules in words are in `../README.md`. Re-export when the canvas changes.
